- Say that your software works with Solstice or Solstice Sync, or that it is
  based on the Solstice source, as plain facts ("compatible with Solstice Sync",

- Use our app icons or logos in your own product's branding.

A fork should have its own name and icon. Changed builds can say "based on
Solstice" in plain text.
